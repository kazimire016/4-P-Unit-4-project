My Unofficial Local Guide to Kazimir Land!

Burrito haven:

A place of pure esctacy where a person can get any burrito of their desire. This is a truly magical face where dreams become a reality and any combination you can think of will be wrapped in a delicious homemade flour tortilla.



Kazimirs fine violins:

Kazimirs fine violins is a great violin shop with access to both of the generous collections o f the violins of the tarisio foundation, and reed yeboah strings.



Mac and cheese clubhouse themepark:

A place of great atractions as far as the eye can see. You may also create a mac and cheese dish of any variety that you can think of.



Order of Travel!!!

  1. Go to burrito haven and dress your palate with the beautiful taste of burritos of every desire.
  2. While you process your food, go and play some violins at Kazimirs fine violin shop and maybe think of taking something out on a trial period.
  3. After this head over to Mac and Cheese clubhouse themepark and hit up some rollercoasters, and get some mac and cheese later for a latte night snack!
